# NBA and NBA 2K verification

For work on MyNBASim, cross-check NBA rules, history, and player data with official NBA or other governing-body sources. For NBA 2K-inspired features, check the relevant edition and mode against 2K material or an identifiable capture of the feature. Record the edition, mode, and roster version where available.

Keep real NBA facts, 2K reference values, and simulator rules distinct. State coverage gaps. A rating database, launch announcement, or unreviewed video does not establish what an in-game screen displays. Exercise the affected screen and game states before reporting completion.

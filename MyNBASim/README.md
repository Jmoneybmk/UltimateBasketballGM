# Ultimate Basketball Simulator

## Franchise hub

The dashboard groups tools into Team, Front Office, League, and Operations. Use **Calendar** to simulate a day, week, month, or the rest of the season, or select a date to simulate through it. Completed team games open their box scores from the calendar. **League leaders** filters by phase, stat, conference, team, position, games played, and player name. Contracts, trades, and releases use the roster table for player selection.

New franchises and subsequent seasons use staggered schedules with 41 home and 41 away games per team. Clubs play each cross-conference opponent twice, each division rival four times, and other conference opponents three or four times. Generated calendars vary by year and retain the older schedule for an existing season.

Regular-season free agency starts with the [November 5, 2026 Hoops Wire report](https://hoopswire.com/2026-nba-free-agents-list/) checked against the roster snapshot. Its player ratings use modeled scouting, with available 2025–26 season stats from [BoxScore Lab](https://www.boxscorelab.com/downloads/) under CC BY 4.0. A signing joins the roster immediately and prorates this year's salary. CPU teams periodically bid for roster depth and use different guaranteed and upfront shares.

Arena tickets can be priced by default or overridden for individual home games. Seating, fan nights, and giveaways affect attendance. Parking, merch, food, and VIP revenue derives from modeled fans, demand, prices, and capacity. Parking and VIP upgrade by level. Merch and food each have ten products, three initially unlocked, with the rest independently purchasable and optional to sell. VIP levels unlock capacity and separately priced packages. New pricing and purchases affect upcoming games.

All teams have 12 staff positions with published coaching names and seeded fictional personnel for other slots. The controlled team can swap current roles or recruit from staff free agency. Simulated staff ratings affect tactics, training gains and fatigue, scouting report detail, and medical recovery. Staff assignments and salaries apply prospectively. Modeled annual salaries appear before hiring and are paid in daily regular-season installments. The budget reserves unpaid staff payroll.

## Negotiations and relationships

Open **Relationships** in the front office or contact a player from their card or free-agency listing. Publicly reported NBA agents provide access until player trust reaches 55. Players without a verified listing have an unnamed representative. Requests depend on the player, the team's contention, and their relationship with the GM. Requests cover the upfront guarantee and a jersey number, a team perk, or another player signing. Active promises are checked against actual agreements and roster state. Commitments last seven regular-season days or two offseason rounds. Keeping them raises trust and breaking them lowers it. An offer must honor any active guarantee, perk, or jersey promise. Each active promise links to the screen where you can act on it. Set a jersey number when making an offer or revising a contract, or change it from a rostered player’s card or the team roster. The game treats #0 and #00 as separate numbers and checks for roster conflicts and pending reservations. Staff give role-specific assessments, and the local beat reporter unlocks out-of-market reporters as their relationship improves.

Open [index.html](index.html) in a browser. Pick an NBA team under Franchise and advance one calendar day at a time. The browser keeps one franchise save locally. Reopen the same page in the same browser profile to resume. Use the League and Players tabs to browse rosters and scouting profiles.

The generated 82-game schedule starts on the roster snapshot date with fresh records and gives teams their own game and rest days. Results use fixed rotations, player ratings, and a saved random seed. Click a game in the franchise log or League Scores to see both teams' box scores. Shooting and other box-score stats are generated from the same save without changing earlier final scores. Once a franchise starts, player profiles and roster tables show only its simulated averages. Shooting percentages use season makes divided by season attempts. The regular season leads into playoffs and season rollover. Rosters, statistics, and attributes are bundled locally. Logos and headshots load from ESPN when online.

## Coaching and health

Open **Set rotation** from the franchise dashboard. Drag players or use the arrow buttons to order the starting five and the bench. Set minutes with the slider or the number field, for a total of 240. Saved changes apply from the current unplayed day. Injury limits reduce projected minutes, with uncovered time assigned to eligible reserves and then the rest of the rotation. A team that cannot cover 240 eligible minutes forfeits.

Fatigue starts at 100 and falls with game minutes, participation, and back-to-backs. A game consumes 3 fatigue plus 0.65 per minute rounded up, with 6 extra for a back-to-back. A day without minutes restores 32 fatigue. After a game, fatigue bands of 80–100, 50–79, 20–49, and 0–19 apply 0, 2, 4, and 6 durability wear. A sore player can play at 50 durability or lower. A hurt player at 30 or lower is capped at 20 minutes and risks worsening. At 10 or lower, players miss at least three weeks, with longer recovery for lower durability. At zero they are out for the rest of the regular season. Training also affects fatigue. Players at zero durability resume recovery when playoffs begin.

## Facilities and player development

Go to **Facilities → Training Facility** from the franchise dashboard. Assign one recurring drill per player, or select Rest. The facility runs drills after normal rest-day recovery. Completed drills add up to 0.15 to the selected attribute and cost 12 fatigue. A player skips training at 50 durability or below or under 40 fatigue after recovery. Training and age-related skill changes carry into upcoming games and player profiles. Existing saves begin tracking development on the next unplayed day.

Peak start, peak end, and annual gain and loss rates are individual game estimates. During peak ages, natural skill gains accrue daily at the player's annual rate. After the peak, natural decline accrues the same way. Drills and age-related changes exclude durability and marketability. Season rollover can adjust 3PT based on makes and misses across consecutive simulated seasons.

**Staff** lists 12 positions across Coaching, Player Development, Scouting, Medical, and the Front Office. The Training Facility pairs with Player Development, the Development Division pairs with Scouting, and the Team Clinic pairs with Medical. The Clinic shows the franchise's injury report and recovery information. The Development Division opens league scouting and roster views.

The Parking Lot, Merch Stalls, Food Stalls, and VIP pages show how facility settings affect home-game revenue. Staff lists coaching names and titles as published on all 30 NBA.com team roster pages for the November 6, 2026 snapshot. Staff salaries are game estimates.

## Finances and contracts

New franchises start with a modeled $330 million bank and published opening annual player salaries, plus a modeled $230.3 million annual league-media distribution that helps support the real payrolls. Existing saves retain their active deals and receive the modeled distribution from their next season. Every player card shows the dated NBA contract snapshot, sourced mainly from [Basketball Reference](https://www.basketball-reference.com/contracts/players.html) and [HoopsHype](https://hoops-hype-us-east1-664912083968.us-east1.run.app/salaries/players/). The snapshot includes available option years and reported guarantees. Rounded salaries and calculated two-way amounts are identified on the card. The simulator applies its own payment terms where a full real contract is unavailable.

Revised roster agreements support flat or escalating salary, one to five years, guaranteed and conditional shares, a configurable upfront guarantee, signing bonuses, performance incentives, and team perks. Current-year salary is prorated, and recording new terms replaces the prior unpaid balance. Team and Solo Panel Ads raise the player's displayed marketability by 3 and 7 respectively, subject to the rating cap. Recorded morale spending awaits a gameplay effect.

Regular-season contract conditions and awards are evaluated at the regular-season close. Playoff conditions settle after the Finals. Only the current year's salary, bonuses, and perks affect the current contract budget. Signing charges the upfront part of that year's guarantee, any signing bonus, and perks. Each continuing year charges its upfront share when the next offseason opens, then pays the remaining guarantee during the regular season. For a fully guaranteed $40 million annual salary with a 25% upfront share, that is $10 million upfront and $30 million in regular-season payroll each year.

## Roster transactions

Use **Roster moves** to propose one-for-one trades through the displayed simulated deadline or release players during the regular season. The receiving team considers roster value and both teams must be able to afford the move. No-trade clauses block deals. The sending team pays any trade kicker on the remaining rollover and conditional salary. The receiving team takes over unpaid rollover and conditional obligations, while previously paid money stays with the original ledger. A release ends both obligations and sends the player to the free-agent pool. Teams keep at least 10 players. Free-agent offers are available in the regular season and offseason.

Moves take effect before the next unplayed game and carry player stats and health forward. The rotation transfers the traded player's planned minutes to the incoming player, and releases redistribute uncovered minutes. Training assignments clear when a player leaves. Completed box scores retain the players who were on the rosters at game time.

## Playoffs, awards, and rollover

The top six teams per conference qualify directly. No. 7 hosts No. 8 for the seventh seed, No. 9 hosts No. 10 in an elimination game, and the loser of 7–8 hosts the winner of 9–10 for the eighth seed. The four best-of-seven rounds use a fixed bracket and a 2-2-1-1-1 home schedule. Regular-season seeding and home-court ties use the [NBA standings tiebreakers](https://www.nba.com/standings). Play-in and playoff games use five-minute overtimes. Play-in, playoff, and regular-season stats are separate. Players with season-ending injuries resume recovery when the first playoff round begins. Seasons that already played under the previous bracket retain that bracket and its awards on replay.

Modeled ballots select MVP, Rookie of the Year, Defensive Player of the Year, Most Improved Player, Sixth Man, Clutch Player, Coach of the Year, Executive of the Year, and the positionless All-NBA, All-Defensive, and All-Rookie teams. The 65-game and 20-minute rules apply only to the specified CBA honors. A season-ending eligibility exception uses a simulated injury forecast. The sim does not have an appeals process. Clutch voting uses modeled scoring events from the box score, with the NBA's final-five-minute and five-point margin criteria. Midseason All-Star selections use separate starter and reserve voting, and the East and West conference finals and the NBA Finals have separate MVPs. Applicable awards and playoff outcomes feed contract payouts.

The game still uses a generated calendar. The All-Star Game, NBA Cup, tracking-based Hustle Award, and player- or community-voted honors that need off-court data are listed as pending. Sources include the [NBA play-in rules](https://www.nba.com/news/nba-adopts-play-in-tournament-on-full-time-basis), [NBA awards history](https://www.nba.com/news/history-all-time-awards), and [NBA–NBPA CBA](https://www.nbpa.com/cba/).

After the Finals, open **Playoffs and awards** to review team options and open next season's free-agent market. Bank balances, continuing contracts, and developed ratings carry forward. Player options and early termination use the next salary relative to the game's modeled market estimate. Expiring deals and declined options enter the unsigned pool. Past seasons retain their brackets, awards, and box scores. Once two simulated seasons are available, rollover compares three-point makes and misses for players with at least 20 games in both seasons and adjusts 3PT by up to two points.

## Free agency and overall ratings

Player overall is the equal-weight average of all 23 current attributes. Team overall is the equal-weight average of the players currently rostered, including injured players. Empty rosters show a dash. The game uses full precision for comparisons and displays two decimal places.

Browse or filter the available-player table and select a player to open their card or make an offer. Submit, edit, or withdraw offers, then resolve the round to compare them against simulated offers from other clubs. Players consider salary, guarantee structure, term, signing bonuses, perks, familiarity with their former club, team overall, and last season's wins. Veterans give contention more weight. Signed deals join the roster and use the same contract ledger. The modeled offseason uses an offer window. Closing the window adds affordable depth to bring teams to the game's 10-player minimum, and AI teams continue making regular-season roster decisions. Conversations provide individualized demands, verbal agreements, and promises.

CPU clubs review free agents weekly and consider roster-fit trades every three weeks through the simulated trade deadline. They respect no-trade clauses, a 90-day restriction on recently signed players, and both clubs' available budgets. They also sign sponsors and invest in training, clinics, and parking. Young CPU players receive individual training on rest days. Each club's facility settings, training costs, staff salaries, and commercial revenue use its own ledger. Completed trades and business decisions appear in Trades & rumors.

## League data

- The league starts from ESPN's November 6, 2026 roster snapshot for all 30 NBA franchises.
- Season tables come from [BoxScore Lab](https://www.boxscorelab.com/downloads/), November 5, 2026 build, under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Imported totals are converted to per-game averages.
- Scouting ratings use the latest completed season with at least 300 minutes when available. Smaller samples move estimates less, and players with no season data use position and measurement baselines. Measurements and box scores serve as proxies for skills such as speed, defensive IQ, and box outs.
- To regenerate the bundled data, save the [ESPN teams feed](https://site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/basketball/nba/teams?limit=40) as `espn-teams.tmp.json`. Save each team's feed from the same API at `/teams/{id}/roster` as `roster-{id}.tmp.json` and the five BoxScore Lab player season totals CSVs as `stats-YYYY-YY.tmp.csv`. Run `node build-roster.mjs` from this folder. The generator validates team coverage, snapshot dates, player IDs, and attributes.

## Design for future installments

- **NPC conversations.** Model individual relationships among GMs, players, staff, agents, reporters, fans, and other entities. Familiarity gates interactions and determines direct or agent-mediated contact. Players make team-specific demands based on contention and relationships. Before free agency or the deadline, the GM can meet demands and give guarantees to improve relationships and signing odds. Broken guarantees cause a major relationship hit. The LeBron example combines salary, playing with Bronny, and housing.
- **Contracts.** Support flat or back-loaded annual salary, guaranteed and conditional pay, years, player and team options, early termination, no-trade clauses, trade kickers, incentives, and cash bonuses. Team perks include housing, transport, security, PR, shared team ads, and solo ads with popularity boosts. Track teammate or family signings, jersey numbers, endorsement pushes, and family jobs as separate player demands.
- **Payments.** Guaranteed salary has an adjustable upfront share paid immediately and a rollover share charged daily. Upfront pay is sunk and remaining rollover is forfeited on trade or cut. Some players demand a higher upfront share, and that share affects the relationship. Conditional pay is due at season end from the player's final team if the contract's requirements are met. Each conditional contract requires at least one condition involving games played, injury absences, eligible awards, minutes, team wins, playoff success, or stat benchmarks.
- **Team money.** One deterministic bank balance receives all revenue and funds salaries, facilities, training, and morale. Spending is limited by affordability. Show payroll expenses daily, weekly, monthly, and annually. GM compensation and personal money come later.
- **Player profiles.** Expand biographies with accolades, career averages and performance logs, wingspan, standing reach, handedness, draft and pre-draft details, and agents. Show relationships and evolving organizations. Actions such as charity donations can improve relationships.
- **Progression.** Preserve decimal changes, including drills such as +0.15 to 3PT. Give each player individual peak ages and gain or loss rates. During the peak, attributes improve, and after the peak, they decline. Compare makes and misses across seasons and allow targeted training to change a player's skill set. Performance thresholds and change sizes remain tunable.
- **Injuries.** Track fatigue from minutes, games, back-to-backs, rest days, activities, and training. Apply durability wear per game at 0× for fatigue 80–100, 1× for 50–79, 2× for 20–49, and 3× for 0–19. At durability 50 or below, a sore player can play through it. At 30, reduce minutes and raise worsening risk. At 10 or below, the player is out for weeks to months or longer. At zero, freeze durability through the regular season and resume recovery when playoffs begin. Later injury types can lock durability for set durations.

### Commercial operations

Open **Advertisement Center** to launch team, solo, or company panels and sign one sponsorship. Choose featured players from the roster. A star identifies contract panel obligations. Active campaigns boost team popularity and home attendance. Company panels earn a home-game fee, and sponsors pay separate home and away rates. The Arena upgrade opens away watch parties with reduced-price admission, concession income, and an operating expense. Finance entries use the settings in effect on the game date.

National media rights credit $230,303,030 per team per season in the franchise model, split evenly between an upfront payment and daily regular-season installments. The NBA announced 11 years through 2035–36, and AP reported $76 billion, equivalent to about $230 million per team per year as a gross equal split. Individual club payouts were not disclosed in the announcement.

Finances lists earned shares of the Player Playoff Pool using published 2025 rates. These are player allocations shown separately from the team ledger. Contractual award bonuses remain payroll expenses.

The current scheduling model assigns individual games to dates using rest, travel distance, home and away stretches, and slate size as constraints. It keeps 82 games and a 41/41 venue split per club, reserves an All-Star break, and starts with a three-game opening slate. Existing saves switch at the next season rollover. The NBA Cup’s result-dependent scheduling is outside this model.

Franchise finances add $85,000 in modeled league commercial revenue per regular-season day and separate club bonuses for standings, playoff progress, and supported awards. The player playoff pool remains separate.

Use **Team → Player editor** to change a franchise player’s profile, assign up to three positions, or edit the 23 ratings. Created players enter free agency, and removed players retain their completed game records. **League → Trades & rumors** shows filterable transactions, injuries, results, awards, and unresolved recruitment stories. Simulation pauses if a controlled rotation needs injury changes. Use **Build healthy rotation** or make manual changes, save, and resume.

Use **Front office → Trades & roster moves** to open the proposal board. The team panels and asset picker reference the supplied NBA 2K26 MyNBA Association captures. Their roster version is unknown. Each of two teams can offer up to six players or picks in any combination. The board displays annual salary changes and projected roster sizes. Future picks start with their original teams in the simulation. Outright trades persist into future drafts, and a team must retain a first-round pick in at least one year of each pair of consecutive future drafts. Protections, swaps, and real-world pick obligations are outside the current pick model.

### SimCast, player development, and the draft

On game day, open **SimCast** from Franchise or Calendar. Choose a play, defense, featured player, and period minutes. Step forward manually or autoplay at 1×, 2×, 4×, or 6×. Save the final to update the same franchise standings, health, finance, and box scores as other games.

**Development Division** uses a library of 1978–2026 historical classes and 2027–2030 projected classes. Choose a source class on Season Handoff or edit, import, and export a class. Beginning in 2031, the defaults cycle from 1978 through 2026. Historical boards cover up to the first 60 picks per year, cross-checked at the first pick against the NBA draft archive. Fictional prospects fill short boards. Ratings and progression are simulator estimates, with rookie statistics and career peak estimates for matching players in the existing career database. The 2028–2030 boards use NBA Draft Room projections, which can change. The picker references the supplied NBA 2K26 MyNBA Eras draft class captures (roster version unknown). Prospect preferences are nonbinding. The draft grants exclusive rights. Sign your drafted players separately in the Development Division. The lottery models the NBA’s 3-2-1 structure, using original franchise picks and modeled rookie salary amounts. Standard-contract players with zero to two service years can be assigned to their affiliate. Three-year players need recorded written consent. Assigned players remain on NBA payroll, sit out NBA games, and earn an attribute boost for every 30 days on assignment.

**Training Facility** schedules weekday practices on non-game dates. At level 3, practice raises pre-peak and peak progression rates by 15% and 25% respectively and lowers post-peak decline by 10% for the practice day. Individual drills stack during the regular season. **Team Clinic** adds recovery bonuses, while the assigned development and medical staff contribute their own separate bonuses.

### Real NBA career records

Open **Career résumé** or **Real NBA game log** on a player card. Real NBA regular-season and playoff records use ESPN's public statistics feeds. The game log includes season archives from 2003–04 through 2025–26 and the 2026–27 snapshot through November 6, 2026. Select an older season to open its local archive. Play-in games from 2020 through 2026 have their own segment. Some historical feeds have fewer game rows than the published season total, and the log displays that difference. The totals view includes source-reported totals only.

Historical honors link to the [NBA award archive](https://www.nba.com/news/history-all-time-awards), official Finals box scores, and dated reports. Dated championship histories reconcile with NBA career title counts for all 577 imported players. NBA 2K27 comparisons cover all 577 players, with the roster capture version and date shown when available. The exact in-game MyNBA Award History layout remains unverified. Franchise statistics and honors are stored separately.

### NBA records and history

The Records and League History layouts reference the supplied NBA 2K26 MyNBA Association captures. The roster version is unknown. Historical Finals and award tables cover their sourced NBA archives through 2025–26. The team career reference uses the five published counting-stat leaders from each official NBA team profile. The retired jersey screen includes published team listings and the NBA-wide Bill Russell retirement. Historical leaderboard coverage remains partial. Franchise game and season results can rank alongside verified entries, while career totals without a compatible historical baseline have their own table.

### Franchise audit

Open **Relationships → Explore relationship network** to browse from any listed player, staff member, representative, team, GM, fan, reporter, or organization. The directory separates sourced family and representation links from simulated familiarity and trust. The [NBA 2K27 MyNBA reveal](https://newsroom-uk.2k.com/news/nbar-2k27-mynba-answers-the-community-with-a-back-to-basics-franchise-overhaul-and-modern-cba-rules) documents GM Trust at the mode level. The relationship directory is a simulator feature. The player biography links to its NBA profile and applicable measurement and shooting-hand sources. Unverified measurements and shooting hands are labeled on the card.

Run `node franchise-audit.mjs all` with Node.js 24 from this folder. The stages run in order through contracts and promises, coaching and SimCast, CPU teams, season transitions, and finances. A stage can also run by itself using `contracts`, `coaching`, `ai`, `transitions`, or `finances`.

The audit uses isolated storage, deterministic seeds, automatic responses to injury rotation alerts, and multiple full seasons. It checks annual payments, promise delivery actions, roster ownership, box scores, archived seasons, playoff and draft transitions, staff payroll, media installments, and club ledgers. The finance stage covers five consecutive seasons for one franchise and two for another. Existing saves begin CPU development and staff payroll at their next unplayed day.
